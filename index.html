6Grammar · have got / has got

Запам’ятай:
  • ✅ I / You / We / They + have got → I have got a hat.
  • ✅ He / She / It (і один предмет чи особа) + has got → She has got a scarf.
  • ❌ заперечення: haven't got / hasn't got
  • ❓ питання: Has Tom got a hat? – Yes, he has. / No, he hasn't.

Обери have або has.
